How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Text content is extracted into a Word-compatible format.
  3. Download the .doc file and edit it in Word or Google Docs.

When it's useful

Useful when you've received a PDF but actually need to edit it — updating an old resume, revising a contract draft, or reworking a document someone sent you as a PDF instead of the source file.

FAQ

Will complex formatting and images carry over perfectly?

Text content transfers reliably; complex layouts, embedded images, and precise formatting may need minor adjustment after conversion.

What file format does it output?

A .doc file, compatible with Microsoft Word and Google Docs.
