Document conversion guide & FAQ

Converting between PDF, Word, Excel, JPG and PNG usually comes down to picking the right tool for what needs to change and what has to stay the same. PDF to Word and PDF to Text pull out the words so you can edit or search them, but neither can rebuild a complex multi-column layout pixel-for-pixel — keep the source file and only convert a copy when precise formatting matters. Word to PDF goes the other direction and is the safer choice when a document needs to look identical on every device, because PDF freezes the layout instead of leaving it to whatever fonts happen to be installed on the reader's computer. PDF to JPG and PDF to PNG render each page as an image — JPG is smaller and fine for photos or scans, PNG is lossless and better for pages with sharp text or line art. Image to PDF and the Document Scanner do the reverse: turning one or more photos into a single, properly ordered PDF, with the scanner adding page-enhancement filters and an optional searchable text layer so the result behaves like a real scanned document instead of just a picture.

Every converter on this page runs the actual conversion inside your browser tab using open-source libraries (pdf-lib and pdf.js) that download once and then execute locally — your PDF, Word file or photo is never uploaded to a Toolnova server. That's also why very large files (several hundred pages, or dozens of high-resolution photos) can take longer or use more memory than a server-side converter would: the work is genuinely happening on your own device.

Frequently asked questions

Will PDF to Word keep my original formatting?

Text and paragraph breaks carry over, but complex layouts — multi-column pages, embedded fonts, tables that span pages — are approximated, not pixel-perfect. For documents you'll keep editing long-term, treat the .doc output as a strong starting draft rather than a final replacement for the source file.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Not directly — decrypt it first. Because the file never leaves your browser, Toolnova has no way to prompt for or store a password on a server; you'll need a PDF that's already unlocked before running it through any converter here.

Why does PDF to JPG produce one image per page instead of one image total?

PDF pages aren't a single continuous image — each page is rendered separately so you can pick and download exactly the pages you need, or grab all of them at once as a ZIP.

Is there a file size or page limit?

There's no artificial cap, but since conversion happens on your device, the practical limit is your browser's available memory. Mobile browsers in particular can struggle with 100+ page PDFs or very high-resolution scans — splitting a large file first with Split PDF often helps.
