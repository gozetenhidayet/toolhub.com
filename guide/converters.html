Converters

Everyday conversions guide & FAQ

Unit Converter covers length, weight, temperature and speed using exact, fixed conversion factors, so its numbers are as precise as your input — there's no approximation beyond normal floating-point rounding. Currency Converter is different: exchange rates move constantly, so the rates built into this tool are static reference points rather than a live feed, useful for a rough estimate but not for anything time-sensitive like an actual transfer or purchase — check your bank or a live-rate source for that. Case Converter and Word Counter are the simpler text utilities in this group: instant case switching (upper, lower, title, sentence case) for writers and developers cleaning up text, and a live word, character and sentence count useful for anything with a length limit, from a social post to an essay assignment.

Frequently asked questions

How current are the currency conversion rates?

They're static reference rates, not a live market feed, so treat the result as an approximation. For an actual transfer, purchase or accounting record, always confirm the live rate with your bank or card provider.

Does Word Counter count differently than Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

Minor differences are normal — tools vary slightly in how they treat hyphenated words, numbers and punctuation when counting. For strict submission limits, use whichever tool your recipient will actually use to check the count.
