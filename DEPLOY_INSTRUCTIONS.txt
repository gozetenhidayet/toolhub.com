IMPORTANT — things this version actually fixes that the prior package did not:
- ads.txt did not exist at all. Without it, Google AdSense can show "ads.txt
  issues" warnings and may limit ad revenue. Now present at the root:
  https://toolnova.tools/ads.txt
- sitemap.xml did not exist — only the human-readable sitemap.html did.
  Google Search Console wants the .xml version submitted directly. Now present.
- robots.txt did not exist. Now present and points to sitemap.xml.
- sitemap.html linked to 20 blog article URLs that did not exist anywhere in
  the previous package — every one of those links was a 404. This is exactly
  the kind of broken-link/crawl-error signal that hurts both Search Console
  health and AdSense's view of site quality. Fixed by replacing that list with
  12 blog articles that actually exist and were verified to load.
- None of the 37 individual tool pages had Google AdSense's ad script, ad
  units, or the Google Translate auto-language widget — only index.html did.
  Anyone landing directly on a tool page from a Google search result (which is
  the whole point of having separate pages) saw no ads and no language switcher.
  Fixed on all 37 pages: AdSense loader script + two commented-out ad slots
  (see below) + full Translate widget + Open Graph tags.
- index.html had regressed to an older base that was missing the
  per-category explanatory content (2000+ words of real, non-duplicated text
  across the 6 tool categories, with FAQs) that was specifically written to
  address AdSense's prior "Low value content" rejection, plus the mobile
  Google-Translate "gap above the banner" fix, plus the H1→H2→H3 heading-order
  fix for accessibility/SEO. All three have been restored.

AD SLOTS ARE INTENTIONALLY COMMENTED OUT:
Every ad placement (2 per tool page, 2 on index.html) is left as an HTML
comment with data-ad-slot="REPLACE_WITH_REAL_AD_SLOT_ID". Do not uncomment
until you have created real ad units in your AdSense dashboard and have their
real slot IDs — a placeholder/fake slot ID causes Google's ad server to
reject the request with a visible error, which is worse than showing nothing.

AFTER DEPLOYING:
1. Open https://toolnova.tools/sitemap.xml directly in a browser and confirm
   it loads as XML with no errors.
2. In Google Search Console → Sitemaps, submit https://toolnova.tools/sitemap.xml.
3. In Search Console → URL Inspection, manually request indexing for your
   highest-priority pages (homepage, merge-pdf.html, pdf-to-word.html,
   compress-pdf.html, split-pdf.html) to speed up initial discovery — new
   pages can otherwise take days to weeks to get crawled on their own.
4. Test several tools on an actual phone and desktop browser after deploying,
   not just locally.
5. Do not request a new AdSense review until: the site is live with this
   update, ads.txt returns 200, sitemap.xml is submitted, and you've spot
   checked a few tool pages actually load content (not just the homepage).
6. Configure Google AdSense Privacy & Messaging/CMP in the AdSense dashboard —
   this is separate from anything in this codebase and must be done inside
   the AdSense account settings.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS:
None of this guarantees AdSense approval on the first re-submission — that
process also factors in traffic history and manual review. What this update
does is remove every concrete, checkable reason in Google's own published
policies for a "Low value content" or indexing rejection: thin/duplicate
pages, broken internal links, missing ads.txt, and orphaned pages with no
sitemap path to them. The rest (traffic building, waiting for initial
indexing) takes real time and cannot be fully compressed into a code change.
