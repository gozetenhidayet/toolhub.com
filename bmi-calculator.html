How to use it

  1. Choose Metric (kg/cm) or Imperial (lb/ft-in) units.

When it's useful

Useful before a doctor's visit so you already know your number, for tracking general trends over time, or just out of curiosity after a health kick.

FAQ

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, so it can be misleading for athletes or very muscular people — it's a general screening tool, not a full health assessment.

What BMI range is considered 'normal'?

For most adults, 18.5–24.9 is classified as the normal range, though this can vary slightly by population and health guideline.
