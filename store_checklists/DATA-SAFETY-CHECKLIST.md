# Data Safety / App Privacy Checklist

Before release, verify every statement against the final app.

## Current starter behavior
- Favorites and recent-tool IDs are stored locally on the device.
- The app opens Toolnova tools only on `toolnova.tools`.
- The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- The UMP consent flow runs before ad requests.
- No account or login is required.
- Full-screen ads are disabled.
- The app does not request all-files access.
- File access remains controlled by the website/system picker.

## Release tasks
- Complete Google Play Data safety accurately.
- Complete Apple App Privacy accurately.
- Add a public privacy policy URL.
- Add a support email and developer website.
- Declare advertising and diagnostics data used by the final SDK setup.
- Re-check every permission generated by plugins.
