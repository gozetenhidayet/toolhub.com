Frequently Asked Questions

Is toolnova.tools really free?

Yes — every tool is free, with no signup, no watermark, and no usage limits.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is ever required to use any tool on this site.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, every tool is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

How many tools are there?

toolnova.tools currently offers 36 free tools across PDF conversion, OCR, calculators, generators, converters and simple browser games — see the full list in our sitemap.

How do you keep this free?

The site is supported by non-intrusive advertising (Google AdSense), which lets us keep every tool free for everyone.

Can I use this in my own language?

Yes — use the language selector in the top navigation bar to translate the entire site automatically.

How can I report a problem or suggest a tool?

Use our Contact page — we read every message.
